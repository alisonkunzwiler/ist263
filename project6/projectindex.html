Why London?

London is one of the most amazing, diverse, and opportunistic cities in the world. From different districts and boroughs, to markets and vendors, to places to travel to from London, it’s all like never before. Everywhere in London is easily accessible by walking, taking the tube, or even a short bus or train ride.

London’s main source of travel is the tube, a public transit system similar to the subway or metro. There are a variety of routes, and trains come every 3-8 minutes, depending on the busyness of the stop. When going to / from areas more on the outside of the city, the tube can be about a 10-30 ride, but once in the center of the city, it is definitely quicker to walk to neighboring areas.

Many would consider Soho to be the area of central London. This is home to the largest shopping strip, and many theaters, sites, and restaurants. Soho sits between the tube stops of Piccadilly Circus and Oxford Circus, and Leicester Square and Covent Garden. All of these areas are hubs for street performers, creative scenes, and dining.

Outer districts such as Hammersmith, Paddington, Kensington, Hampstead, and so many more offer more peaceful, home neighborhood feelings. With hidden gems all over the city, it is important to not leave out some of these less popular districts. Similarly, London is known for its vast amount of known parks and outdoor areas, though the popular ones are not necessarily as peaceful as the hidden ones. Some of the parks are considered sites for tourists to visit, and oftentimes are located by some of London's most historic pieces. No matter what time of day you head to relax in some greenery, other people are always there too.

London also serves as a major travel hub, making it easy to visit nearby cities and towns for day trips. Exploring London means you also need to experience some of the other beauties of England. Known places like Oxford, Cambridge, and Windsor make for a perfect day trip to explore beautiful architecture and historic sites. Brighton, the Cotswolds, and Seven sisters offer scenic views and coastal cottage vibes when you need to get away from the bustling city life.

One of the most memorable things about London is how active it always feels. At any time of the day, people will be at cafes, pubs, or out on the streets. Spending time in public spaces is the way of living. This constant sense of activity and accessibility makes London feel lively and social in a way that is very different from other places.
