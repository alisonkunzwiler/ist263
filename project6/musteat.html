Must Eat

With such a diverse population, London offers an incredible dining experience. Most notably, they are known for their fish n chips, which are a must try. Additionally, locals and tourists have adapted the Sunday Roast, a traditional afternoon meal featuring a variety of foods. From fast food to upscale restaurants, in my opinion these are places you must visit.

Rias Pizza

Located in notting hill area, highly rated pizza, served Detroit style.

Soft Serve Society

Located in seven dials market, offers delicious ice cream and treats.

Bills

A popular London chain restaurant, offers a diverse menu, I loved the $5 unlimited pancake Fridays.

Swiss Butter

Another must try, meals come with fries, salad, bread, and a steak or fish of choice. Really delicious and a great price.

Camden Market

Churros Donut

Yummy donuts

The Cheese Wheel

One of the only places where I found alfredo pasta, made in a cheese wheel.

The Cheese Bar

Grilled cheese restaurant with a variety of different cheese and snacks.

Funky Chips

An absolute must try, loaded french fries with immense amounts of flavor. Don’t let the line scare you, it moves quick!

More Eats From Anywhere

Udderlicious Ice Cream

Located in Covent Garden, really good ice cream with a number of flavors that are changed out. Really good with allergies as well.

Burger and Lobster

Probably the best caesar salad I’ve had. Another chain, but really good food.

Dishoom

One of the most popular Indian restaurants in London. A favorite among my friends.

Flat Iron

Another popular London chain, known for its steak dinners.

Borough Market

Ginger pig

Notable sausage rolls

Strawberries

Popular chocolate covered strawberries, but worth it.

Raclette

Melted cheese dishes, always a hit.

The Black Pig

Known for its delicious sandwiches.

The Bath Dairy

Farm fresh mac n cheese

Fish!

One of the best places to try your fish and chips!

More Eats From Anywhere

Honest Burgers

A quick food vibe, absolute best hamburgers.

Poppies fish and chips

The most known fish and chips, has a number of stores.

Humble Crumble

Yummy custard desserts that are almost pie-like but served in a cup.

The Ivy

Various locations, classic british american dining.

This list offers a variety of different dining options. While these recommendations are a great starting point, part of the experience is trying new foods, waiting in line for something you’ve never heard of, and finding your own favorites along the way.
